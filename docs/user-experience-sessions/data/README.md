# Fixtures for the simulated user sessions

Realistic data for three simulated users. Each user opens an app that already holds about 8 weeks
of history (23 Aug to 27 Oct 2026). Everything is generated by `gen.mjs` and checked by
`check.mjs` with the app's own parsers, so a row is what the app itself would have written.

## Files

| File | What it is |
|------|------------|
| `mother.json`, `employee.json`, `merchant-manager.json`, `merchant-building.json` | Sheet data: `{ "<tab title>": [[cell, ...], ...] }`, header row first, every cell a string. Pass with `--data`. |
| `sms-mother.json`, `sms-employee.json`, `sms-merchant.json`, `sms-merchant-building.json` | Pending bank SMS: `[{ id, sender, body, receivedAt, source }]`. Pass with `--sms`. |
| `init-mother.js`, `init-employee.js`, `init-merchant-manager.js`, `init-merchant-building.js` | Optional `--init` scripts. They only edit `localStorage` after the runner's seed (see "Init scripts"). |
| `sms-balance-chain.json` | Generated sidecar for `check.mjs`: the role of every SMS in the «مانده» chain (`recorded`, `pending`, `pending-off`, `redelivery`). The app never reads it; do not pass it to `--sms`. |
| `gen.mjs`, `lib.mjs`, `areas.mjs`, `smsFormats.mjs`, `mother.mjs`, `employee.mjs`, `merchant.mjs`, `building.mjs` | The generator. Edit these, never the JSON. `areas.mjs` holds the builders of the module tabs (vehicles, goals, vault, timesheets, dang-split, net worth, recurring, reminders). |
| `check.mjs`, `checkAreas.mjs` | Validator (see "Regenerate and validate"); `checkAreas.mjs` is the part that checks the module tabs. |

Pairs to run together:

| Session | `--data` | `--sms` | `--init` |
|---------|----------|---------|----------|
| Mother | `mother.json` | `sms-mother.json` | `init-mother.js` |
| Employee | `employee.json` | `sms-employee.json` | `init-employee.js` |
| Shopkeeper, shop books | `merchant-manager.json` | `sms-merchant.json` | `init-merchant-manager.js` |
| Shopkeeper, building he manages | `merchant-building.json` | `sms-merchant-building.json` | `init-merchant-building.js` |

The building is a separate spreadsheet inside the app, so it is a separate run: the mock workbook
is then treated as the building (`init-merchant-building.js` marks it as a building and the tab
`تنظیمات دوره` carries the matching `نوع فضا` row and the manager role of `perf@test.local`).
`sms-merchant.json` holds only the SMS of the shop accounts; the building fund's SMS are in
`sms-merchant-building.json` (a real phone gets both, but each workbook only knows its own accounts).

## Units, calendar, formats (all read from the code)

- Money: **toman**, plain numbers as strings (`"850000"`, no separators). The mock settings use `currency: "toman"`.
- Bank SMS print **rial** (x10). The saved templates have unit `rial`, so the app divides by 10.
- Dates in cells (`تاریخ`, check dates, receivable/debt dates, instalment start): **Gregorian ISO `YYYY-MM-DD`**.
  The app shows them in Jalali. Charge months, budgets, anchor month and recurring `lastPosted` use Jalali keys `YYYY-MM`.
- Created-at cells (`زمان ثبت`): exactly what the app writes, `toLocaleString('fa-IR')` in the Tehran zone (Persian digits, Jalali date).
- Today is 2026-10-27 = 5 Aban 1405. The app's "this month" is the Jalali month, so **this month has only 5 days of data (23 to 27 Oct)** and "last month" (Mehr, 23 Sep to 22 Oct) is full. Shahrivar is the first month.
- Ids are deterministic UUID-shaped strings; SMS ids are `sha256(body)` like the native plugin.
- Month anchor: `تنظیمات دوره` holds `ماه شروع محاسبه = 1405-06` and `تاریخ عضویت = 2026-08-23`; `موجودی ماهانه` holds the opening balance (sum of the accounts' opening balances). Wallet balances, `گردش حساب` and the records agree to the toman (checked). Savings-goal deposits are the exception: they are `گردش حساب` lines with no income/expense row, and the check counts them.

### SMS balance chain

The app sets an account's balance to the «مانده» of the newest SMS you confirm (`nextAccountBalance` in `bankSmsBalance.ts`), so the printed balances must be what a real bank would print or the tester sees balance jumps that only the fixture causes. Per wallet account that has SMS:

- The balance in the data file is the balance **after every already-recorded event**. The pending SMS are real transactions it does not include yet.
- **Already recorded** SMS ("already in books") print the balance right after their own record. This is read from the books (the `گردش حساب` line of that record), because other, non-SMS records move the same accounts; check.mjs matches each one to that ledger line.
- **Pending** SMS, oldest first: «مانده» = previous «مانده» +/- own amount (debit minus, credit plus), starting from the wallet balance. Confirming them in time order moves the balance by exactly each amount and the jump notice does not appear. An own-account transfer (employee, 5M) follows the same rule on each of its two accounts.
- Every recorded SMS is older than every pending one on its account (otherwise both timelines cannot be true). To get that, the generator moved some pending SMS later: mother's 3,000,000 withdrawal and its 2,500 fee are now on 25 Oct at 19:20 and 19:21 (were 22 and 21 Oct); in the building the unknown-card deposit moved from 24 Oct 19:05 to 26 Oct 11:05 and unit 7's 4.8M from 25 Oct 12:10 to 26 Oct 13:10. Nothing in the books changed; the duplicate/new/unknown tallies per file are the same as before.
- A body delivered twice (the salted `#2` copies) repeats its original's «مانده»: confirming the copy does not move the balance again.
- Bank figures are printed in rial (x10); the chain is computed in toman.

**Intentional stale/out-of-order «مانده» (one per dataset, to test the jump notice).** It is the newest pending SMS of one account and its «مانده» is **+1,000,000 toman** too high, so confirming it makes the balance move by the amount plus 1,000,000:

## Init scripts

The runner seeds settings whose category lists have only two options per form. The app copies the
category tab into settings only when some pages (records, debts, receivables, settings) are
opened. The init scripts put the lists from the tab into the saved settings up front, set
`currency: "toman"` and the debt/receivable category lists. The personal inits also copy the
reminder and the four vehicle category lists (periodic, deadline, mechanic, expense).
The building init also marks the workbook as a building.

**There is no switch that enables vehicles, timesheets, dang-split, the vault, goals or reminders.**
The app has no module flag in the settings: in a personal workbook these pages are always in the menu
and read their tabs (a building workbook is created without them). So the init scripts set nothing
for them, and the data in the tabs is all it takes.

## Regenerate and validate

```bash
node --experimental-transform-types docs/user-experience-sessions/data/gen.mjs
node --experimental-transform-types docs/user-experience-sessions/data/check.mjs
```

Node 22.18+/24 is needed. The scripts import the app's TypeScript read-only (a tiny resolver hook is
registered in `lib.mjs`); nothing under `src/` is touched. `check.mjs` verifies the SMS balance chain (every pending SMS, run through the app's real `nextAccountBalance` in time order, must move the balance by exactly old +/- amount except the one declared +1,000,000 exception per dataset; every recorded SMS must print the balance of its own ledger line) and: tab names come from
`sheetNames.ts`; headers equal the app's headers; all cells are strings; dates parse; categories exist
in the category tab; ledger chain, wallet balance and records add up independently; checks, debts,
receivables, instalments, counterparties, budgets, recurring items, building units/stays/profile parse
with the real row parsers; payment links point at existing records; each SMS runs through the real
`matchSms`, and "already in books" uses the real duplicate rule (same account, direction, amount, +/- 1 day).
`checkAreas.mjs` adds, per dataset: every module tab is present with a minimum number of rows; headers equal the app's; ids unique; vehicle rows round-trip through the app's own serializer (`vehicleRowFromImportCells`), services have next km = km + interval, every expense id points at a real «خودرو» expense with the same amount, fuel meta litres x price = amount, mileage never decreases with the date and the profile holds the highest reading; goals parse with `rowToGoal`, their deposits are in the ledger, balance <= target and status = `nextGoalStatus`; vault assets round-trip through `rowToVaultAsset`/`vaultAssetToRow`, transactions name catalog assets and never sell more than is held, holdings through `computeHoldings` with an empty tgju answer; timesheet entries parse with `rowToTimesheetEntry`, do not overlap and have the right duration; dang-split shares (`splitExpenseShares`) add up to every amount, `buildGroupSummary` balances net to minus the fund deposit and the settled flags follow `setDangSplitPersonPaid`; net-worth rows parse and assets - liabilities = net; recurring items parse (`rowToRecurring`) with a known account and category; reminders round-trip through `personalReminderRowFromImportCells`; budgets are expense categories; every counterparty named in a record exists; a building workbook carries no personal-only tab. The wallet re-derivation counts goal ledger lines.
To move the history, change `TODAY` in `lib.mjs` and rerun.

## Doubts and assumptions (not resolved from code alone)

1. **Nothing was loaded into the running app.** Correctness is established with the app's parsers and serializers, not in a browser. Run one smoke screenshot per file first.
2. **Clock.** The app reads the real clock. If the sessions do not run on 2026-10-27, install a fake clock in the page or regenerate with another `TODAY`; otherwise "overdue", "due soon" and "this month" shift.
3. **Time zone.** Created-at cells and SMS `receivedAt` are Tehran-time instants. A browser in a very different zone can move an SMS to the neighbouring day. Melli bodies print a Jalali date and the app trusts it (within 7 days); Mellat bodies print none, so the received day is used.
4. **"Bounced" check:** the app has no bounced status (a check is paid or not). The 12-days-overdue received check carries the text `برگشتی` in its subcategory (free text) as a stand-in.
5. **Repeated SMS body:** the native plugin derives the id from the body, so a real repeat has the same id. The repeats here get a distinct id (`sha256(body + "#2")`) so they stay two cards; check.mjs counts them as "salted".
6. **Building expenses:** the managed lists (expense kinds, payers) are not seeded, so `نوع هزینه` is empty, `پرداخت‌کننده` is `fund`, and no expense is split into unit shares. Only fund-paid expenses exist.
7. **Building duplicates:** many units pay the same 2.4M on adjacent days, so the SMS duplicate rule flags new 2.4M deposits as "already recorded" (4 of 6 are). This is real app behaviour, left in on purpose; it is not a fixture error.
8. **Cross-workspace behaviour** (the shopkeeper's personal books and the building in one account: routing of SMS by card between workspaces, bridge between books, `accounting_workspace_cards_v1` cache) is not covered; the mock supports one spreadsheet at a time.
9. **Not filled:** the activity log, reminder rules (`یادآوری`), push subscriptions. IBAN and card colours are left empty. Attachments (receipt photos) are empty.
10. **Opening-balance chain:** the monthly net of records (what reports use) differs from the wallet movement only by the one deliberate "no effect on balance" record (employee, 1.1M).
11. **Card numbers, phone numbers, bank sender codes and names** are invented. SMS wording follows formats in `bankSmsFixtures.ts` (Melli, Mellat) and the bank-SMS parser; real banks vary.
12. **Mother and employee have no building data**, and the employee has no receivables or debts (not requested).
13. **Treasury prices.** A built-in asset has no price in the sheet: «سکه امامی», «نیم سکه» and «دلار» are priced from tgju at run time. When the mock answers `{}` every such holding is worth 0 toman and the vault total, the net-worth "assets" and the dashboard ignore it. So the merchant's catalog tab (`دارایی_صندوقچه`) holds a row for `sekee` (142,000,000) and `usd` (155,000) with source «manual» and the manual price dated 2 days ago (`rowToVaultAsset` reads «manual» as manual even with a tgju key). «نیم سکه» is left on tgju on purpose: it shows no price when the network is mocked. The silver asset is a user asset (id is a UUID), manual price 246,000 per gram dated 48 days ago, so the app flags it as stale (more than 30 days). The app seeds the other built-in rows on first open of the vault. Prices are toman per unit, quantity x price is the cost; the sell row only needs a price for the realised gain.
14. **Vehicle tabs:** the app's row functions for services, deadlines, visits, history and fuel meta are private, so `areas.mjs` mirrors their column order (the header order) and writes what the app writes (an expense «<type> — <vehicle>», a «mileage» line when the reading is new, a «periodic» line per completion; a deadline renewal leaves a «deadline» line; an old service typed in afterwards has no expense, no reading and an empty expense id). Only the profile row goes through the app's serializer. The plate is stored as `serial|letter|number|province`. Days-before only takes 0, 1, 2, 3, 7 in the forms (the older shop reminder with 5 was changed to 7).
15. **Timesheet money:** there is no rate or amount column; the rate is text in the description and the income is an ordinary income record. «تایید شده» is the only link between hours and an invoice.
16. **Dang-split is a calculator:** nothing posts to the wallet or to the income/expense tabs, so the trip does not show in the employee's balances.
17. **Net worth** values are made up for the history (see above); the app recomputes the current month itself and would rebuild missing months from the records with different numbers.
18. **Goals** are not records: no `درآمد`/`هزینه` row exists for a deposit, so reports of income and expense do not show them; the account history does (source «هدف پس‌انداز»).
19. **Mother opens with 55M on the card** (the first fixtures had 15M) because the car (about 15M), the goals (28.5M) and the pending SMS debits would not fit otherwise.
